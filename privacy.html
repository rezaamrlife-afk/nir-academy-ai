Privacy Policy

NIRA Academy AI is an AI-powered educational platform designed to help teachers create exams, prompts, and learning materials. This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your data.

We do not collect: precise location, financial information, contacts, photos, or biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the NIRA Academy AI platform
  • To authenticate your account securely
  • To store and retrieve your saved prompts and content
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties.

4. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Saved prompts and content are retained until you delete them or your account
  • After account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days
  • Anonymous usage analytics may be retained for up to 12 months

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • In-app: Go to My Profile → Delete Account to permanently delete your account and all associated data
  • By email: Send a deletion request to info@niracademy.com

We will process your request within 30 days and send a confirmation email.

6. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text
  • Database access is restricted and monitored
  • We conduct periodic security reviews

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@niracademy.com.

8. Children's Privacy

NIRA Academy AI is designed for educators and adult users. This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it immediately. If you believe a child has submitted data, please contact us at info@niracademy.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. International Users & Legal Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data. Our legal basis for processing is your consent and the performance of our service contract with you.

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what data we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of sale (we do not sell data).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@niracademy.com.
